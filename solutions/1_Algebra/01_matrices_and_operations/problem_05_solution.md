## Matrix-Vector Multiplication

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
x=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### Solution

$$
Ax=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2\cdot 4 + (-1)\cdot 2 \\
1\cdot 4 + 3\cdot 2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 - 2 \\
4 + 6
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### Result

$$
\boxed{Ax=\begin{pmatrix}6 \\ 10\end{pmatrix}}
$$
