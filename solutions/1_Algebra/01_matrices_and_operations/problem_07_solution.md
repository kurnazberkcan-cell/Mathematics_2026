## Results

$$
AI = A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
IA = A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A + 0 = A =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
A^2 =
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 4 \\
0 & 4
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A^3 =
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 12 \\
0 & 8
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### Roles of $I$ and $0$

- $I$ is the multiplicative identity: $AI = IA = A$.
- $0$ is the additive identity: $A + 0 = A$.

### Pattern in powers of $A$

$$
A^n =
\begin{pmatrix}
2^n & n\,2^{n-1} \\
0 & 2^n
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The diagonal entries double with each power, and the upper-right entry follows $n\,2^{n-1}$ (1, 4, 12, ...).
