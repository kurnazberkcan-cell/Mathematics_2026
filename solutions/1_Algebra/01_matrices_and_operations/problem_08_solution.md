## Results

After $R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 2R_1$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & -1 \\
0 & 0 & 3 \\
-1 & 1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

After $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 + R_1$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & -1 \\
0 & 0 & 3 \\
0 & 3 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
$$

After interchanging $R_2$ and $R_3$:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & -1 \\
0 & 3 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The final matrix is upper triangular (row echelon form).
