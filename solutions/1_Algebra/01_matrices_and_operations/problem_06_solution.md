## Results

$$
A^T=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 4 \\
2 & 5 \\
3 & 6
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B^T=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & -1 \\
0 & 1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 11 \\
8 & 23
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
(AB)^T = B^TA^T =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 8 \\
11 & 23
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Hence $(AB)^T = B^TA^T$ holds in this example.
