**Formula:** For a $2\times 2$ matrix, $\det\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc$

**Matrix 1:**

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix}
= 3\cdot 4 - (-2)\cdot 5
= 12 + 10
= 22
$$

**Matrix 2:**

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix}
= 1\cdot 14 - 7\cdot 2
= 14 - 14
= 0
$$

The second determinant is $0$ because the second row is $2$ times the first row, so the rows are linearly dependent and the matrix is not invertible.
